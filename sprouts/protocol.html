Lot tracking

  • Write down the lot number from each seed bag when it arrives, along with the date received.
  • Keep the supplier's test results or certificate of analysis for each lot, if provided: [where these are filed].
  • Record the seed lot on every batch in the batch log and on every label.
  • If a supplier recalls a lot, stop using it and check the log for any batches made from it.

Optional: hydrogen peroxide seed soak

  1. Warm 3% hydrogen peroxide to about 140°F (check with a thermometer).
  2. Soak the measured seed in it for 5 minutes, stirring gently.
  3. Drain and rinse thoroughly with clean, filtered water before the regular soak.

4.Sanitizing

Every jar, lid, sleeve, tub, insert, cover, and tool, between every batch:

  1. Wash in hot, soapy water. Scrub mesh lids and colander holes with a brush.
  2. Rinse off all soap.
  3. Sanitize in 1 Tbsp of plain, unscented bleach per gallon of water. Mix fresh each time. Contact time: [per bleach label, e.g. 1–2 minutes].
  4. Air dry fully on a clean rack. Don't towel dry.

Hand washing

  • Wash hands with soap and warm water for at least 20 seconds before handling seed, sprouts, or clean equipment.
  • Wash again after touching raw meat, pets, garbage, or your face and phone.
  • No growing or handling while sick.

Work surfaces

  • Clean and sanitize the counter area before starting a batch.

5.Growing

  • Rinse 2–3 times a day with filtered water, swirling so every seed gets fresh water.
  • Drain completely after every rinse. Jars sit upside down at an angle; tubs lift out to drain. Standing water is the enemy.
  • Temperature: keep the growing area at a steady 65–75°F. Room thermometer location: [where].
  • Light: keep covered while sprouting; [any light exposure before harvest].
  • Small batches only, so nothing sits crowded and damp.

6.Harvest & storage

  1. Final rinse in cold, filtered water. Remove loose hulls if needed.
  2. Dry in a clean salad spinner until the surface is dry to the touch.
  3. Pack into clean, labeled containers.
  4. Refrigerate right away at 41°F or below. Check the fridge thermometer: [how often].
  5. Hand off within 1–2 days of harvest. Anything older gets composted.

Discard anything slimy, discolored, or off-smelling, at any point. When in doubt, throw it out.
